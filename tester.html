EXO PLANET #6 - EXhOrizon in JAKARTA

EXO will embark on their sixth solo concert tour following the release of their eighth full-length album, REVERXE. EXO PLANET #6 - EXhOrizon will begin with a three-day concert in Seoul from April 10 to 12 before continuing its global journey across 14 cities worldwide, including a stop in Jakarta on June 6 and June 7.

 

Since their debut in 2012, EXO have defined a generation of K-pop through their distinctive sound, powerful performances, and strong artistic identity. Beginning with “MAMA,” the group has delivered a string of major hits including “Growl,” “CALL ME BABY,” “Monster,” “Ko Ko Bop,” “Tempo,” and “Love Shot,” sparking what became widely known as the “EXO syndrome.”

 

In 2013, their first full-length album became a million-seller, marking the revival of Korea’s million-album era for the first time in 12 years amid a declining physical market. EXO later became the first artist to debut in the 2000s to surpass 10 million cumulative album sales in Korea. By 2026, they achieved their eighth career million-seller with their eighth full-length album, reaffirming their lasting popularity and commercial power.
 

The group is also widely recognized for its signature universe concept, where each member possesses a distinct superpower—an expansive narrative woven through their music, visuals, original content, and the long-running concert tour brand “EXO PLANET,” launched in 2014. Through this cohesive storytelling, EXO continue to captivate a passionate global fanbase.
 

In 2026, EXO marked a powerful “return of the kings” with the release of their eighth full-length album, REVERXE, further expanding their universe-driven storyline. Released two and a half years after their previous project, the album drew global attention, earning recognition from Forbes as one of the “Most Anticipated K-Pop Comebacks of 2026” and inclusion in NME’s “Most Anticipated Album Releases of 2026,” underscoring EXO’s continued global impact and relevance.
